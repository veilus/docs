## System requirements

| | Windows | macOS |
|---|---------|-------|
| **OS** | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (x64) | macOS 13 or later |
| **Processor** | x64 | Apple Silicon (Intel Macs are not supported) |
| **Network** | Needed to download the browser engine and to activate a license | Same |

Veilus is available for Windows and macOS only.

## Download

Get the installer from **[veilus.io/download](https://veilus.io/download/)**:

- Windows: `Veilus_<version>_x64-setup.exe`
- macOS: `Veilus_<version>_aarch64.dmg`

All releases are also listed on [GitHub](https://github.com/veilus/releases/releases).

## Install

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Windows">

<Steps>

1. Run `Veilus_<version>_x64-setup.exe`.

2. If Windows SmartScreen shows **Windows protected your PC**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

3. Follow the installer, then open Veilus from the Start menu.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="macOS">

<Steps>

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Veilus** into **Applications**.

2. Open Veilus. The app is not notarized by Apple, so macOS says it cannot verify the developer. Click **Done**.

3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**, then confirm **Open Anyway**.

   Later launches open normally.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Download the browser engine

The installer does not include the browser. Profiles cannot launch until an engine is installed.

<Steps>

1. In Veilus, open **Settings → Engine & updates**.

2. Under **Chromium Version Manager**, the list of available versions loads automatically. If it is empty, click **Sync from Cloud**.

3. Click **Download** next to the version marked **Latest**.

   The first engine you download becomes active automatically. **Engine in use** under **Updates** shows which build profiles will use.

</Steps>

To switch versions later, download another version and click **Activate**. **Cleanup Old** deletes downloaded versions that are not active. The Free plan keeps one engine version at a time; paid plans can keep several.

## Updating Veilus

The app does not update itself. To update, download the latest installer from [veilus.io/download](https://veilus.io/download/) and install it over the current version. Your profiles, scripts and settings are kept.

Engine updates are separate: download a newer version in **Settings → Engine & updates**.

## Where Veilus stores data

Profiles, scripts, settings and downloaded engines are stored in a `.veilus` folder in your home directory:

- Windows: `%USERPROFILE%\.veilus`
- macOS: `~/.veilus`

## Uninstalling

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Windows">

1. Open **Settings → Apps**, find **Veilus** and click **Uninstall**.
2. To also delete profiles and engines, delete `%USERPROFILE%\.veilus`.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="macOS">

1. Quit Veilus (**Quit Veilus** in the menu bar icon, or <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>Q</kbd>), then move **Veilus** from Applications to the Trash.
2. To also delete profiles and engines, delete `~/.veilus`.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::caution
Deleting the `.veilus` folder removes every profile, including cookies and logins. Export anything you want to keep first.
:::
