## Create and launch a profile

<Steps>

1. **Start a new profile.** Click **New profile** at the top of the sidebar. The **Create Profile** dialog opens.

2. **Pick the OS, language and region.** Under **Operating System**, choose Windows or macOS. It defaults to the OS of your computer, which is the safer choice: a profile for a different OS has to imitate more, so Veilus shows a warning when you pick one. Under **Language & Region**, quick-select a market or set the language and **Timezone** yourself. Click **Next**.

3. **Organize.** Optionally add tags so you can find the profile later. Click **Create Profile**.

   Veilus generates a fingerprint that matches the OS you chose. The new profile appears in **All profiles**.

4. **Launch it.** Click the launch button on the profile's row (**Launch browser**). In the dialog, keep **Browser Only** selected and click **Launch 1 Profile**.

   A browser window opens with that profile's fingerprint, proxy, cookies and storage. Click the same button (**Stop browser**) to close it.

</Steps>

To add a proxy, click the profile's row to open its panel and go to the **Network** tab: choose a **Proxy Pool** or fill in **Manual Proxy**. **Match to proxy** measures where the proxy exits and sets the profile's timezone and language to match; save to keep the change. See [Proxy setup](/profiles/proxy/).

If **Settings → Timezone check** is set to **Block** (the default), a profile whose timezone does not match where its proxy exits will not launch; Veilus shows both values so you can fix one.

## Test the profile

Tick the profile's checkbox and click **Test** in the selection bar. Veilus opens the profile on a set of fingerprinting test sites. The **Score** column then shows how many sites passed, for example `5/6`. Click the score to see the **Test report**, or open the profile's **Test** tab.

If you change the fingerprint or proxy afterwards, the score is marked as out of date: test again.

## The profile list

**All profiles** shows one row per profile: **Status**, **Name**, **OS**, **Created**, **Last Used**, **Proxy** and **Score**.

- **Search and filter:** press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on macOS) to jump to the filter bar. Save a filter you use often; it appears under **Saved filters** in the sidebar.
- **Profile panel:** click a row to open its panel. Tabs: **Overview**, **Fingerprint**, **Network**, **Data** (cookies, snapshots, dataset), **Automation**, **Extension** and **Test**. **Duplicate** is in the panel header; the copy gets a new fingerprint with the same OS, language and timezone.
- **Several profiles at once:** tick their checkboxes, then use the selection bar to **Launch**, **Assign Pool**, **Assign dataset**, **Test**, or **Delete**. Deleted profiles go to **Trash**, where you can restore them.

Up to 16 browsers can be open at the same time, whether you open them by hand, from a schedule, or through the API.

## Free plan limits

The Free plan includes 5 profiles on 1 device. Creating several profiles at once (**Batch create**), Veilus Flow, schedules, Veilus Sync and the local API/MCP need a paid plan or the 7-day trial. See [Plans & license](/reference/plans-and-license/).
