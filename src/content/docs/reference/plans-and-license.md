## Plans

| | Free | 7-day trial | Monthly | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Profiles | 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Unique fingerprint per profile | Included | Included | Included | Included |
| Installed engine versions | 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Latest engine | Included | Included | Included | Included |
| Veilus Flow, schedules, batch runs, script templates | Not included | Included | Included | Included |
| Veilus Sync, import/export | Not included | Included | Included | Included |
| Local REST API & MCP | Not included | Included | Included | Included |
| Devices | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1, 5, 10 or 20 |
| Duration | Forever | 7 days | While subscribed | Forever |

"Batch runs" covers both running a script on many profiles at once and creating several profiles in one go (**Batch create**).

Prices and checkout are on [veilus.io](https://veilus.io/). In the app, click your plan at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Plan** to compare plans and pay in VND or USD. For more than 20 devices, email for a quote.

## 7-day Pro trial

1. Open **Plan** or **Settings → License** and click **Try Pro free for 7 days**.
2. Enter your email and click **Send**.
3. If the email has not used a trial yet, you receive a Pro key valid for 7 days. Paste it into the key field in the same dialog and activate it.

There is one trial per email and per device. When the trial ends, Veilus returns to the Free plan; your profiles stay.

## Activate a key

Open **Settings → License**, click **Activate License Key**, paste the key and confirm. Each key covers a fixed number of devices.

If activation says the key is already active on the maximum number of devices, contact support to free up a device, then try again.

**Deactivate** switches the current device back to the Free plan.

## When a plan ends or you go over the limit

Nothing is deleted. On the Free plan, Veilus opens 5 profiles; the others show as locked until you upgrade or delete other profiles. Paid features (Veilus Flow, schedules, Sync, API & MCP) are unavailable until you renew.

For a key with an expiry date, the plan badge at the bottom of the sidebar shows how many days are left. If your key renews by bank transfer, Veilus shows a banner with a **Renew** button before it expires.

## Add a device

Lifetime keys can buy extra devices: **Settings → License → Add a device** shows the price per extra device.

## Refunds

Monthly payments can be refunded within 7 days, lifetime licenses within 14 days. See the [refund policy](https://veilus.io/refund/).
